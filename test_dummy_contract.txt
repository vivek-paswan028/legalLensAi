NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between Alice Corp ("Disclosing Party") and Bob LLC ("Receiving Party").

1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party.

2. Obligations
The Receiving Party shall not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party and shall use it solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship.

3. Term
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of 5 years from the date of disclosure.

4. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
